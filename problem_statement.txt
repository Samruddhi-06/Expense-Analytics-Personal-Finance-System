Project 5 — Expense Analytics & Personal Finance System
Problem Statement

Build a Python-based personal finance system that stores and manages transaction history and analyzes spending patterns to help users understand their financial behavior.

The system will use a transaction dataset (CSV) as its historical data source and allow users to:

Add, view, update, and delete expenses.
Store transaction details such as date, category, amount, description, and payment method.
Validate and clean transaction data.
Analyze spending using Pandas.
Calculate financial KPIs such as total spending, average transaction, category-wise spending, and monthly spending.
Generate meaningful visualizations and insights from the transaction history.
Final Workflow

          ↓
